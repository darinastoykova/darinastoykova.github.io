About Me

My name is Darina (Darry), I design intuitive and accessible digital products. My approach to each challenge is with resolve and an open mind. With a background in graphic design, I like to make people feel neat about concepts and ideas.


I build responsive cross-platform design systems for B2B and B2C operating models. As a professional, my work is primarily that of aligning user-centered design methodologies with business needs.
